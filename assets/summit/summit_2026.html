2026 State Demography Summit

The State Demography Summit is an annual one-day conference presented by the State Demography Office. Each year, the event features presentations by State Demography Office demographers and economists on the latest Colorado population and jobs estimates and forecasts. Attend the summit in-person or via the live-stream.

The 400+ in-person and virtual audience includes:

  • State and local government officials, staff, planners, and economic developers;
  • Real estate professionals and property appraisers; and
  • Members of the business community and general public.

This year’s summit will features the release of DOLA’s Statewide Housing Needs Assessment, a presentation by Steve Colella, Chief of Staff for Douglas County School District, presenting about the county’s rapid growth and shifting birthrates, and Hanna Scovill, Vice President and Senior Economist for Pacey Economics, discussing Colorado’s economic and competitive outlook.

The 2026 State Demography Summit will be held on Friday, November 6, 10:00 a.m. to 3:15 p.m., at the Douglas County School District Legacy Campus (10035 S. Peoria St., Lone Tree, Colorado).

Doors open at 9:30 a.m.
